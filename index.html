About

My professional work involves building applications around complicated rules, data, and integrations. Beyond the applications themselves, I’m drawn to the mechanisms underneath: execution, coordination, state, and the trade-offs that shape an architecture.

I’m also drawn to generative computation — simple local rules producing organic, unexpected form. This site collects experiments and investigations as they develop.
